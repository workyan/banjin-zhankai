Sheet Metal Layout Calculator

Calculate cones, bends and transitions with clear flat-pattern diagrams.

Sheet Metal Layout Calculator helps fabricators, sheet metal workers and ductwork installers check development dimensions on site.

CHOOSE A SHAPE
Work with concentric and eccentric cone frustums, single bends, segmented elbows, intersecting round pipes and square-to-round transitions.

ENTER DIMENSIONS WITH CONTEXT
Use illustrated parameter positions to enter diameters, heights, thickness, bend angles and offsets. Input checks flag invalid dimensions before calculation. Length inputs use millimetres, centimetres or metres.

REVIEW THE RESULTS
For supported shapes, inspect development dimensions, true lengths, numbered points and clear diagrams. Cone calculations show slant length, sector radii, sector angle and theoretical area. Single-bend calculations include bend allowance and bend deduction using a K factor.

SAVE AND REUSE
Keep calculation history on your device, save supported results to a local project and return to recent inputs. Copy or share result text through the system share sheet.

WORK OFFLINE
Core calculations do not require an account or network connection. No ads or subscriptions are included in the current app.

This app provides ideal geometric calculations for layout assistance. Results do not automatically include seams, weld gaps, cutting kerf, springback or process allowances. Diagrams are not 1:1 cutting templates. Check the drawing, material, thickness and fabrication process before cutting.
